Battery Voltage: This is typically at register 0x3100 or 0x3200, depending on the model.

Battery Current: Register 0x3102 or similar.

State of Charge (SOC): Typically found around 0x3203 or 0x3204.

Battery Capacity: Can be fetched from register 0x3300 or 0x3301.
